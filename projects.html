BSc Principles of neurotransmission by biogenic aminesBachelor's thesis · University of Zagreb, Faculty of Science · Mentor: A. Maršavelski 2019 ›
Question

How does a signal cross the synapse in neurons that use biogenic amines (dopamine, norepinephrine, epinephrine, histamine, serotonin) as messengers, from synthesis of the transmitter to its removal?

Approach

Literature review of the complete neurotransmission cycle.

Outcome
  • A structured synthesis of the cycle: synthesis of catecholamines, indolamines and imidazolamines; vesicle loading by VMAT; Ca²⁺-triggered exocytosis; receptor binding and receptor subtypes; deactivation by reuptake and catabolism.

FR GacS–RetS: crystallising a bacterial virulence switchResearch placement · AFMB, Marseille · 525 h · Lab of Florence Vincent 2021 ›
Question

In Pseudomonas aeruginosa, the kinase GacS and its inhibitor RetS act as a switch between chronic and acute infection. How does RetS act on GacS, and how do the open and closed conformations of GacS differ?

Approach

Protein production and crystallisation screens of GacS in open and closed conformations, with and without RetS, aimed at a structure.

Outcome
  • Crystals from these screens supported later discoveries in the group. Those results are not yet published.
MSc Two cancer-associated proline mutants of human DPP IIIMaster's thesis · University of Zagreb · Lab for Protein Biochemistry and Molecular Modelling · Mentor: M. Matovina 2022 ›
Question

Human DPP III is a peptidase that also binds Keap1 and so influences the Nrf2 oxidative-stress pathway. Do two mutations found in tumour samples (P358L from melanoma, P360S from ovarian cancer) change its activity, and the behaviour of the loop it uses to bind Keap1?

Approach

Site-directed mutagenesis, expression and purification, fluorimetric enzyme kinetics, and AMBER MD simulations of wild type and both mutants.

Outcome
  • Both mutants were produced and purified, and both have a lower specificity constant than wild type (higher KM, lower turnover).
  • The global fold is preserved in simulation, but the loop carrying the Keap1-binding ETGE motif is more flexible in P358L: a stabilising Gln484–Thr475 hydrogen bond is present in ~17% of frames, against ~93% in wild type.

GZ DPP III and the Keap1 Kelch domainResearch placement · University of Graz, Institute of Molecular Biosciences · 720 h · Host: Tea Pavkov-Keller 2022 ›
Question

Does either mutation change how tightly DPP III binds the Kelch domain of Keap1, the interaction that stabilises Nrf2 and can raise chemotherapy resistance in tumour cells?

Approach

Isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC) of wild-type and mutant DPP III against the Kelch domain.

Outcome
  • P358L binds Kelch roughly 4–5× more tightly (Kd ≈ 150 nM) than wild type (≈ 690 nM) or P360S (≈ 550 nM), consistent with the extra loop flexibility seen in simulation.
ITC runs showed partial protein precipitation and an apparent stoichiometry below 1:1, so absolute thermodynamic values are approximate. The Kd trend is the result.
PhD Weak RNA–protein interactions across scalesPhD · Max Perutz Labs, University of Vienna · Zagrović group · FWF ELLIPSE 2026 ›
Question

Is the specificity of RNA–protein binding in unstructured contexts partly set by the intrinsic affinities between individual nucleotides and amino acids, which would tie binding to the structure of the genetic code (the complementarity hypothesis)?

Approach

A multi-pronged study at four length scales:

Monomers · DOSY NMR

Can single amino-acid/nucleotide affinities be measured in solution?

Nucleotide monophosphates shift amino-acid diffusion coefficients by more than the variability of the internal standard. A simplified geometric model resolves binding preferences, and a complete 4×20 affinity set looks feasible.

DOSY method page →
Oligomers · ultra-high-density peptide arrays

Do weak monomer preferences add up in short peptides?

Relative affinity patterns were reproducible across RNA concentrations. Single amino-acid contributions strongly predict oligomer binding, with the strongest binders deviating from simple additivity.

Peptide-array method page →
Protein · FUS and FMRP

Do the mRNA's composition profile and the protein's affinity profile line up?

Strong correlations along the sequence (FUS purine content vs guanine-affinity profile, R = −0.84). Full-length FUS was expressed and purified; low RNA yield from in vitro transcription blocked the quantitative binding assays.

Protein production page →
Simulation · MD and free-energy perturbation

Can nucleotide perturbations in RNA–protein complexes be compared systematically?

The FUS ZnF–RNA complex was stable and became the model system. The result is a validated, scalable free-energy workflow for nucleotide perturbations.

MD & FEP page →
Outcome
  • The thesis neither supports nor rejects the complementarity hypothesis. It shows that weak, additive RNA–protein interactions are measurable and reproducible, that no single universal nucleotide/amino-acid affinity matrix holds across contexts, and it leaves a toolbox of methods for testing the hypothesis further.
