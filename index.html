Experience

2022 – 2026
Vienna
Doctoral researcher — protein–RNA interactions
University of Vienna / Max Perutz Labs, Vienna BioCenter

A multiscale programme on the physicochemical principles of protein–RNA binding, run as four connected workstreams and coordinated across approximately five specialist groups and several institutions (Max Perutz Labs, BOKU, KIT, University of Arizona, Vienna BioCenter Core Facilities).

  • Monomeric-scale amino-acid / nucleotide interactions by DOSY NMR — experimental and model design, sample preparation and analysis, with measurements performed by NMR specialists.
  • A custom 1,445-sequence peptide-array system across multiple RNA conditions, with a full high-throughput QC and additive-modelling (BVLS) pipeline.
  • FUS and FMRP sequence profiling, construct design, protein production, RNA production and biophysical workflow development — including an approximately tenfold yield improvement on one construct.
  • A custom GROMACS/SMArt RNA nucleotide-transformation free-energy framework, plus ~1 µs equilibrium MD per complex across three structured FUS systems.
  • Python and HPC-based analysis, QC and automation workflows throughout.

The methods did not converge on a single universal answer. The thesis instead identifies the boundary conditions under which each holds — a more defensible, context-dependent framework.
